Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

MindBite respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when using our content publishing automation tools and related services.

1. About MindBite

MindBite is a content page focused on mindset, psychology, self-improvement, human behavior, relationships, and life lessons. We use automation tools to help prepare and publish content to our Facebook Page.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the services and permissions enabled, our automation system may process Facebook Page identifiers, Page access tokens, content, captions, images, publishing status, and technical information required to operate the service.

We do not intentionally request personal Facebook profile information from visitors simply to view our public content.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to create, manage, and publish content to the MindBite Facebook Page, maintain the automation workflow, diagnose technical problems, and protect the security of the service.

4. Third-Party Services

Our publishing workflow may use third-party services, including Meta Platforms, Facebook Graph API, n8n, Cloudflare Workers AI, Google services, and other providers configured by the page owner. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Meta Privacy Policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

Cloudflare Privacy Policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

5. Data Sharing and Retention

Information may be processed by the service providers needed to operate the workflow. We do not sell personal information. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal obligations.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect access tokens and other configuration information. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Privacy Choices

If you have a privacy question or believe that information associated with this service needs to be corrected or deleted, please contact the page owner using the contact information provided below.

8. Children's Privacy

This service is not intended to collect personal information directly from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when our services or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the MindBite Page owner through the official MindBite Facebook Page.
